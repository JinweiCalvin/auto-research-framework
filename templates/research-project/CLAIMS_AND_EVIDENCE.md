# Claims and Evidence

Keep one row per independently testable claim. Candidate claims from Idea2Paper remain hypotheses until supported by cited literature or reproducible experiment output.

| Claim ID | Exact claim | Type | Evidence artifact + locator | Counterevidence / scope | Status | Planned test / owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Status: `candidate`, `unverified`, `supported`, `mixed`, `falsified`, `not-tested`.

Each numerical claim should identify the result file, row/field or figure-generation script. A literature claim should point to the cited passage or section. Keep expected results separate from observed results.
